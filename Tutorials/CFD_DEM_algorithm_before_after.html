REEF3D CFD–DEM Algorithm: Before vs After (Top‑Level DEM Interface)

This page visualizes the coupling algorithm around sixdof_cfd::start_cfd and collision handling. Before: collision is computed by sixdof_collision directly from 6DOF. After: 6DOF calls a top‑level dem interface, and solver‑specific adaptivity lives in dem_cfd.cpp (and dem_nhflow.cpp for NHFLOW).

Before (current branch): CFD↔Collision inside 6DOF Collision owned by sixdof_cfd

In the current structure, sixdof_cfd instantiates sixdof_collision and calls calculate_collision_forces() directly. Adaptivity (detection thresholds + contact model switch) is embedded inside sixdof_collision.

motion/integration DEM collision/contact CFD coupling / forcing

After (target): CFD calls DEM interface (dem_cfd) Adaptivity in dem_cfd.cpp

After the refactor, sixdof_cfd depends on a top‑level DEM interface. The CFD‑specific DEM implementation (dem_cfd.cpp) owns the adaptive policy: it chooses both (1) detection algorithm and (2) contact force model per pair/contact. Shared mechanics remain reusable (grid, SAT, history, force kernels).

Tip: if you want collision forces to be computed on the most up‑to‑date pose, you can place the DEM call after the stage’s integration and transform update, but before mesh/raycast (depends on your coupling choice and stability constraints).
